What's New

Product updates and release notes for Housemore.

0.4.0 July 2026 Latest

New

  • Reviews — clients can rate and review a completed visit and revisit them in a "My Reviews" screen; ratings roll up to the provider.
  • Account deletion — self-serve account deletion from Settings, on mobile and web.
  • Booking reminders — automatic, timezone-aware reminders for clients and team members.
  • Regenerate brief — team members can refresh a visit's "Know Before You Go" brief from the latest household details.
  • Notification bell (web dashboard) — an unread badge and a dropdown of recent notifications.

Improved

  • Account security — sign-in and permissions moved to a hardened, token-based model.
  • Recurring bookings — more reliable scheduling across mobile and web.

Fixed

  • Maps & distance — client addresses are located correctly again, so map, distance, and on-site check-in work as expected.
