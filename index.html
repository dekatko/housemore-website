The Real Problem

You're not losing clients
because of bad work.
You're losing them to bad handoffs.

When a team member changes, everything about that household disappears with them. The preferences, the access details, the note from last visit. New person, blank slate — and the client feels it every time.

🔄
Team Handoff Blindness
When a different team member covers a job, they arrive knowing nothing about that household. The client re-explains everything. Again.
🧠
Knowledge That Walks Out the Door
Every preference and relationship detail lives in one person's head. When they leave, you start over with that client from scratch.
📋
Bookings Without Context
Your team sees a job at 123 Main St on Thursday. Nobody sees what happened last visit, what was flagged, or what that household needs.
The Platform

Built around the household,
not the booking.

Every client home gets a living profile. Every visit adds to it. Every team member shares it.

🏠
Household Profiles
Every client home gets a living profile — preferences, access notes, service history, and team context. One record, shared by your whole team, that grows with every visit.
📝
Post-Visit Notes
After every visit, capture what happened, what was flagged, and what to remember. The next team member reads it before they walk in the door.
🔄
Team Handoff Intelligence
When coverage changes, context doesn't get lost. Every household preference and history travels with the job — not the person who used to do it.
🗓️
Booking Studio
Drag-and-drop weekly scheduling in the web dashboard. Assign jobs across your team and locations, with conflicts and time-off flagged as you build the week — and every booking carries the household's context.
📍
Check-In & Task Checklists
Staff check in on arrival via geofence, work through household-specific task lists, and check out — all tied to that household's permanent record.
💰
Payment Tracking
Log payments, track outstanding balances, and see revenue at a glance. Always know exactly where your money stands.
📥
Leads & Inquiries
New booking requests land in one inbox. Triage leads, follow up, and convert them into clients and scheduled jobs — without losing a single one.
🌐
Online Booking Page
Give clients an embeddable booking link. Requests flow straight into your inquiries inbox — no phone tag, no missed jobs.
🏢
Team & Multi-Location
Manage staff, availability, and pay across one location or many. Smart Replacement finds qualified cover the moment someone calls out.

How It Works

From client list to household knowledge in one afternoon.

1
Add your households
Import your client list and each home gets a profile — address, preferences, access details, and service history. Ready before the first visit.
2
Build the relationship record
Every visit adds to the household profile. Notes, flags, preferences — your whole team shares one memory of every client home.
3
Every visit starts prepared
When a booking is assigned, the team member sees the household's full history. They walk in knowing exactly what this client needs — even if they've never been there before.
4
Handoffs that don't cost you clients
When coverage changes, the new team member inherits everything. No re-introductions, no dropped details, no lost relationships.
